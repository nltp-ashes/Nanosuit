## **v0.8.0 - 09/07/2025 - Alpha Build**

[![Downloads](https://img.shields.io/github/downloads/nltp-ashes/Nanosuit/v0.8.0/total?label=Downloads)]()

**• Additions :**
> • Added corners on the top left and top right of the HUD as in Crysis;

**• Changes :**
> • Made it so the suit turn off sound effect always plays when entering malfunction state;
> • Changed (only for DirectX 10 & 11) the shader used for cloak mode with a way better one (Thanks LVutner);

**• Bug fixes :**
> • Fixed a memory leak causing the nanosuit code to keep running after removing the suit;
> • Fixed tacmap always displaying, even when the minimap setting was set to "disabled";
> • Fixed malfunction not properly restoring after loading a save;
> • Fixed energy not always restoring correctly after loading a save;

**Note:** v0.8.0 and future versions will contain a custom shader, which means you now have to clear your shader cache when installing/updating/removing the addon.
